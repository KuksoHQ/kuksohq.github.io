Gyrolog

Log your life.

Gyrolog helps you record what happened, see your week as it was, and find a next step that fits your life. It is in private Preview on iPhone and is not publicly available.

In the Preview

Start with what happened.
Decide what to adjust.​

01

Capture

Type or say what happened. Gyrolog proposes Diary entries, and you review, edit, save, or discard them.

02

This week

Each day sits on the Day Dial at the times you logged, beside your Goals and the direction you set for the week.

03

Weekly review

Look back through Drift, Pattern, and Plan cards that cite your own entries. You accept or correct each one.
