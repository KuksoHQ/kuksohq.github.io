The product direction beneath the conversation

Your life has context.
Keep it connected.​

Gyrolog’s product direction brings together three foundations. These describe the system being built, rather than a claim that every capability is available in the current Preview. Useful history belongs in your records, beyond any one conversation.

What happened

Diary

A history of moments, observations, and decisions you can return to without reconstructing the week from memory.

What stays useful

Collections

Keep references, ideas, wishlists, and other information together when their value is about a topic rather than a date.

Where you want to go

Goals

Name a direction in your own words, then connect progress to the records that support it, with a clear reason for what counts.

Our initial focus: people whose learning, work, and personal projects span several tools. Bring daily records and chosen goals together so the next review starts with context.

A possible reading to review

DriftCompare intention with reality
You made time for two short sessions. The longer session you planned was postponed.
PatternA reading you can challenge
A small starting point after dinner may fit your current evenings better. Three notes suggest a possibility, not a fixed trait.
PlanAn adjustment you choose
If that reading fits, try one short lesson after dinner next week. Adjust the direction rather than creating a backlog to repay.

Carry the learning forward.

A completed review is intended to bring the useful summary and your agreed plan into the next week. Earlier interpretations remain open to revision; a remembered pattern still needs fresh evidence.

A few clear answers

Where Gyrolog stands.

Is Gyrolog available now?+

Gyrolog 0.13.0 is a private iPhone Preview that its maintainer has tested. It includes Diary capture and review, Pilot conversations, and Navigator sessions. Gyrolog is not publicly available, and there is no public sign-up.

Does Gyrolog decide what I should do?+

No. Its AI may offer a draft, a question, or a possible pattern. You decide what is accurate, what to keep, and what to do next.

How is this different from keeping notes?+

The private Preview brings Diary records together with Pilot conversations and Navigator sessions. The broader product direction is to connect what happened with personal memory, chosen direction, and reflection.

Who is Gyrolog being built for?+

Our initial focus is people balancing learning, work, and personal projects whose notes and goals are scattered across tools. Gyrolog is being built to connect their daily records with reflection and a manageable next step.

Where does Claude fit?+

Claude is part of our development workflow. Product integration remains planned and is not included in the current Preview.

How will my information be handled?+

Diary notes are stored on this iPhone and may be included in iPhone Backup. Coaching & voice processing is off until you opt in; Gyrolog sends information when you ask Pilot or Coach a question, or ask it to split a voice capture. A random install ID, token counts, and estimated cost are kept for up to 62 days to support allowance and cost displays. AI providers process requests and may use prompt caching. Turning processing off stops future requests; it cannot recall information already sent.

What if AI or the network is unavailable?+

Saved Diary records remain on the iPhone. Pilot and voice processing need a network and an enabled Coaching & voice processing setting, so those requests are unavailable while either is off.
